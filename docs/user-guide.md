# CampusLib — User Guide

This guide explains how to use the CampusLib application as both an Administrator and a Student.

---

## 1. Administrator Guide

Administrators have full control over books, members, and library loans.

* **Logging In:** Enter your admin credentials on the login screen.
* **Managing Books:**
    * Navigate to the **Books** screen from the sidebar.
    * Click **"+ Add Book"** to register a new book into the system.
    * Use the **Edit** or **Delete** buttons on any book row to modify or remove book records.
* **Managing Members:**
    * Navigate to the **Members** screen.
    * Click **"+ Add Member"** to register a new student account.
* **Processing Returns:**
    * Navigate to the **Loans** screen.
    * Click **"Process Return"** to record when a student brings back a borrowed book, updating the available copies automatically.

---

## 2. Student Guide

Students can browse the catalog and borrow available books.

* **Logging In:** Enter your student credentials on the login screen.
* **Browsing & Searching Books:**
    * Go to the **Books** screen to view the library catalog.
    * Use the search bar and filter options (Title, Author, ISBN, Category) to find specific books.
* **Borrowing a Book:**
    * Click the **"Borrow"** button next to an available book.
    * Confirm the borrow action. The due date is automatically set to 14 days from the borrow date.
* **Tracking Loans:**
    * Navigate to the **Loans** screen to view your active and past book borrowings.
